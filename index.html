How the numbers are calculated

Only stock buys and sells are used; option rows and entries with no symbol are skipped. Each fill's net cash amount from Fidelity's Amount column is used, so commissions and fees are included. Fills are matched first-in-first-out per symbol, and positions can carry across days. Closed trades are counted per closing fill. Drawdown is measured on end-of-day realized equity against the running peak (never below starting capital). Margin interest, borrow fees and other non-trade entries are not included.

Fidelity history has dates but no times. Within a day, fills follow the file order (newest first is detected automatically). Totals are unaffected, but individual trade results can shift.

Check the net P&L and the "sum of all fills" line against your Fidelity statements. If positions show as unmatched, the export likely starts after they were opened.
